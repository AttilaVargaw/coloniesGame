### Aerlion
#### Description
"Food Basket of the Colonies" Aerilon, or Aerelon, is a temperate world in the Cyrannus star system, and one of the Twelve Colonies of Kobol.
Sharing an orbit with Canceron and Hestia, Aerilon is located at the gas giant's L4 Lagrange point.
At some point following the colonization of Gemenon and the other Colonies, Aerilon was colonized. Much of the planet's landmass is within the temperate zone and the mild climate, along with its moderate precipitation made it ideal for agriculture. Covered in rolling hills and prairies, many farms and livestock ranches were established on its surface and the planet become known for being the "food basket for the Twelve Worlds". Outside of its capital city of Gaoth and Cuffle's Breath Wash, no other large settlements are known to exist at this time.
Noted for its vast fields, prairies, and plains. The planet is not massively fertile, requiring extensive, labor-intensive cultivation and work to grow its colossal food stocks. As a result, Aerilon's population has a reputation for being rugged, working-class, and no-nonsense
Much of Aerilon's economy is geared towards agriculture and, consequently, a great deal of its population lives on farmland. Many of Aerilon's inhabitants tend to be poor and relatively uneducated, with a reputation of being hard workers and plain speakers. The belief of self-reliance is deeply ingrained within most of Aerilon's citizens and their local planetary government does not support or establish welfare programs for those in need and political dissent is not tolerated.
Outside of farming, the planet's other major export is minerals, with substantial mines located in several locations the University of Aerilon and Promethea A&M were the two centers for higher learning on the colony, with the former teaching the arts, and the latter teaching business skills. How many graduates were expected to earn a living on Aerilon is not known, though it is known some Aerilonians as Dr. Gaius Baltar migrated to other colonies for work.

### Aquaria
"The Ice Colony" Aquaria, also known as Aquarion or Aquarius, is a planet in the Cyrannus Star System, and one of the Twelve Colonies of Kobol, orbiting Helios Delta. A sparsely populated planet, though travelers and tourists could swell this number twentyfold. There was no capital city, though Heim is the largest settlement. Both Aquaria and Canceron share an orbit with the gas giant Hestia and are placed at the L4 and L5 Lagrange points, respectively.
Aquaria was one of the latest, if not the last, of the Twelve Worlds to be settled. Unlike worlds such as Caprica, which were settled as colonies from the start, Aquaria was originally a research outpost that, over time, developed into a distinct colony. Unlike the Virgons, who were a pre-existing group that settled several planets in the Cyrannus Star System, Aquaria's population largely consisted of migrants from other colonies who sought to live in the frontiers of some of the last settled locations in Helios Delta.
Aquaria's political system was based on a direct democracy system, whereby all Aquarians had the right to vote on community matters rather than electing a representative and could even elect a Prime Minister based on a popular two-thirds majority vote.
Aquaria is a cold planet with an icy ocean that covers most of its surface. The only major landmass, Kyrios, is a crescent-shaped continent with several volcanoes.

### Canceron
"The Largest Democracy" Canceron is a planet in the Cyrannus star system, and one of the Twelve Colonies of Kobol, orbiting Helios Delta. Canceron shares an orbit with Aerilon and Hestia, occupying the L4 and L5 positions.
Canceron has the largest population of the Twelve Colonies and is a temperate world with extremely large cities. However, despite its population Canceron has been unable to convert that into economic success and several of Canceron's massive cities are surrounded by extensive slums. Canceron's remoteness from the other major colonies may be responsible for its seeming inability to get a large-scale industrial, military or tourism economy established.
History
Canceron was colonised some time before or during the 11th century, being under a democratic system of government for a thousand years before the Fall of the Twelve Colonies. It was largely ignored by the imperial superpowers, Virgon and Leonis, but remained economically behind as a result of its distance. The development of faster-than-light propulsion was a boon to Canceron, as it and neighbouring Aerilon could profit on the extraction and shipping of minerals from themselves and the gas giant, Ragnar.
Perhaps thanks to its fertility, it boasted a large population.
Canceron was founded as a constitutional democracy, with a planetary congress representing some eighty-eight regional states.

### Caprica
Known as "Capital of the Colonies" and also originally known as Capricorn, is a planet in the quaternary Cyrannus star system, orbiting the star Helios Alpha in a shared orbit with Gemenon. Caprica was one of twelve worlds in the system to be colonized by humans native to the distant planet Kobol, founded by those of the Capricorn tribe. While Caprica remained underdeveloped for centuries, it eventually became the focal point of technological and societal advancement in the Twelve Colonies.

As an independent colony, Caprica was led by a Prime Minister, and was plagued by terrorist attacks orchestrated by monotheistic groups opposed to the perceived decadence of Caprican society.
Caprica was possibly the first planet colonized by the Twelve Tribes after the settlement of Gemenon. In the first centuries of expansion, Caprica lagged behind Virgon and Leonis, which held colonial empires that dominated the region. When the two empires exhausted one another in a protracted interplanetary war, Caprica began its rise as the new dominant power.[1] On the eve of the Cylon War, Caprica became the undisputed center for culture, art and education, and became the richest and most economically and technologically developed of the Twelve Colonies, as well as the most powerful militarily and saw heavy migration from people from various planets seeking to start a new life. At the start of the war, Caprica was federated into the United Colonies of Kobol and hosted its president in Delphi.
Central in the Trojan orbit, Caprica is a beautiful blue-green world with the perfect climate. The colony has rolling hills, pleasant lakes, and deep forests. The cities are heavily populated, but it never seemed that way. Glittering silver towers spike through bustling open-air markets and plazas. Parks, rivers, and waterfalls are everywhere. So comfortable are Caprica's cities that people rarely paid attention to the steady lines of vehicles streaking past overhead.
The city of Delphi houses all three branches of government and hosts the biggest corporations and universities in the system. The city has replicas of the Forum, Temple, and Opera House trying to replace the originals left behind on Kobol. The Museum of the Colonies displayes many original artefacts from the lost home world. Other major cities include Pheobus, and Qualai, which is the site of Qualai University.

### Gemenon
Known as "The First Colony" is a planet in the Cyrannus star system orbiting the star Helios Alpha, known for its extreme climate, ranging from frozen plateaus to searing deserts. Gemenon was one of the poorer colonies and was also considered the most religious, with several major centers of religious worship. It is a close neighbor of Caprica, with which it shares an orbit as a double planet.
Gemenon was the first planet in the system settled on by the Kobol refugee convoy. In the aftermath of their arrival, the colonists endured a technological dark age where computer information access was lost and all information became passed on written paper. As consequence, basic information such as why the colonists left was interpreted over the next two thousand years as a religious document and taught by priests.
Gemenon is known for the Gramadas, a vast mountain range that can be seen from space.

### Leonis
"Heart of the Colonies" Leonis is a highly temperate and physically largest world in the Cyrannus star system, and one of the Twelve Colonies of Kobol, orbiting Helios Beta. The patron god of Leonis was Artemis, the daughter of Zeus and sister of Apollo. Unlike other colonies which had evolved Caprican as their standard language, even in the 19th century Leonis' official language was Leonese. Leonis is noted for its wine, ski slopes, and its centers of education and learning.
In the centuries following Gemenese dark age and the colonisation of other worlds,[2] Leonis and Virgon emerged as two powerful empires which controlled several other planets.[1] Many other planets were colonised by the two, spreading their culture across the four star systems. A number of conflicts took place over control of these planets, including Libran, Picon, Sagittaron, Scorpia, Tauron and Troy.[1] Fighting on Tauron took place around the late 11th century, and resulted in an independent Tauron run by Tauronised settlers.[3] Both empires would finally collapse in the 18th century, with Leonis becoming a republic, and while in the 19th century Leonis' relations with Virgon had warmed, its relations with the formerly Virgon-aligned Picon were still problematic.[1][4]
As Leonis has almost no axial tilt, its climate is mild and predictable. Beaches towards the equator remained warm, while mountains to the poles remained cold, allowing it to have year-round tourist destinations. Because of the warm weather around the tropics, Leonan wine was also grown well and was considered to be of fine taste.
In the 19th Century (100s BCH), Leonis had two major cities - Luminere and Hedon - of which the former served as its capital. Hedon itself became a popular tourist destination across the Twelve Worlds because of the Grand Casino, while the Royal Palace of Leonis was an important cultural attraction

### Libran
"The Colony of Justice" Libran is a planet in the Cyrannus star system, and one of the Twelve Colonies of Kobol, orbiting Helios Gamma. It and Aquaria are the only two of the Twelve Worlds to not have fixed capitals.
Libran was underdeveloped for much of its existence until some 200 years before the Cylon War, when Caprica made efforts to create a Colonial Government. Due to its undeveloped and neutral nature, Libran was selected to be the home of the Inter-Colonial Court. This court was located in the city of Themis, its only major settlement. Themis was Libran's de facto capital, there being no legal status for one on the planet. This court was subsequently able to settle disputes between the Twelve Worlds.
Libran is known for its dense jungle environment, though its stormy climate made it an unpopular choice for colonization, and development on the colony was limited. In the time of the Colonial Government, it was considered a planet-sized wildlife reserve.
The Grand Library of Libran was an architectural landmark on the planet.

### Picon
Picon, known as "The Ocean Colony" is a planet in the Cyrannus star system, and one of the Twelve Colonies of Kobol, orbiting Helios Alpha. It is named after the Pisces tribe who resided on Kobol, though its citizens may not have been affiliated with that tribe. The planet itself is 75% ocean, but unlike Aquaria, its climate is temperate and its seas support an abundance of aquatic life.
Following the re-development of space technology by the peoples on Gemenon, the planets Caprica, Picon, and Virgon were among the earliest established colonies, each under the administrative control of the Virgonese.[2] Because of this mutual evolution all three were culturally similar and spoke the same language. Following its 18th-Century (200 BCH) decline, Picon gained independence from Virgon though maintained close ties.
Three-quarters of Picon's surface is water, which earned it the name "The Ocean Colony" and is abundant with aquatic life. The average temperature on Picon is lower than that of Caprica.
In the 20th century, Picon was known for its socially-progressive attitudes towards health care and affordable housing. Its crime rate was relatively low and boasted an affluent and educated population. Following the Cylon War, the Colonial Fleet made its base in Perkinson, with Picons representing a large representation of administrative staff.

### Sagittaron
Sagittaron is a planet in the Cyrannus Star System, orbiting Helios Gamma.[1] It is named after the Sagittarius tribe who resided on Kobol, though its citizens may not have been affiliated with that tribe.

Sagittaron was colonized by Gemenese fundamentalists who felt their brothers and sisters there were not truly devoted to the sacred texts. The Helios Gamma system, also home to Libran and Scorpia, were occupied by the imperial superpowers Virgon and Leonis, who exploited the system for centuries. Sagittaron gained its independence following the two empires' mutual destruction.
With the formation of the United Colonies of Kobol two centuries later, Sagittaron became more exposed to the wider human society. However, racism against Sagittarons existed even after then, as their opposition to military service offended the wider community in the Cylon War and their views on medicine stereotyped them as walking plagues. Criticism of Sagittaron's incorporation into the United Colonies continued twenty years after the end of the Cylon War, and Sagittaron Tom Zarek led a terrorist campaign against the government, where he gained sympathy from idealists.[3]
Sagittaron was considered by the other colonies to be a barren, mountainous world, and much of its population was spread out into isolated valleys, such as the Acheron River. The only known major city was Tawa.
The Sagittaron culture, like the Gemenese polytheists, believed in the literacy of sacred scriptures, but there were enough disagreements that prompted them to abandon the planet entirely. Among the cultural beliefs was that relying on modern medicine was in some way sinful, and Sagittarians preferred herbal folk remedies.

### Scorpia
"Playground of the Colonies" Scorpia is a planet in the Cyrannus star system, and one of the Twelve Colonies of Kobol, orbiting Helios Gamma. It is named after the Scorpio tribe who resided on Kobol, though its citizens may not have been affiliated with that tribe. The planet was known for its ring system, created by a broken-up moon some 100,000 years before the planet was settled.
Scorpia's foundations are unknown, but like the other planets in the Helios Gamma system, it became a battleground in the colonial proxy wars between Virgon and Leonis, two Helios Beta planets that possessed planetary empires. These imperial conflicts ended sometime around the 200 BCH, and the colonies became more welcoming of interplanetary travel. In regards to culture, Scorpians placed a great deal on religious toleration and family orientation, with Dionysus as their patron god. By the time of the Fall of the Twelve Colonies, Scorpia had reached a population of 450 million.
Before the Fall, Scorpia was a verdant jungle world, noted for its dense foliage; the towering Guardian Mountains, and the tropical Argentum Bay.
Using the planet's rings, Scorpia's industry was geared towards mineral extraction and the construction of ships in orbital yards.
On the planet itself, Scorpia also had a large tourism sector due to its environment and became a popular vacation resort in the 200 years of colonial stability before the Cylon War. It was known in the Twelve Worlds for two major cities, Celeste and Argentum, of which Celeste was also the planet's Capital.

### Tauron

Tauron, known as "The Old Colony" is a planet in the Cyrannus star system, orbiting Helios Alpha. It was originally named Taurus, after the Taurus tribe who resided on Kobol, though its citizens may not have been affiliated with that tribe.

Following the conclusion of the Gemenese dark age and humanity's reinvention of space flight, Tauron was colonised for use in the rearing of livestock, with the city Minos starting out in the middle of a cattle trail. Not as aggressively developed as other worlds of the time, Tauron suffered from famines brought on quickly as a result of conflicts, forcing people to eat dirt to survive. The term "dirt-eater" would live on as a racial slur against native Taurons, known as Ha'la'tha in their native language.
Starting from the 12th Century (800 BCH), Tauron endured colonisation by Virgon and Leonis, which were expanding their interplanetary empires.Though Tauron independence would resume in the mid-18th Century (2000 BCH) in a popular revolt known as "Our Day", cultural distinctions between the native Taurons - the "Ha'la'tha" - and Tauronised settlers continued to exist. The government of Tauron was predominantly represented by the latter community, who had strong ties with neighbouring worlds such as Caprica.
An uprising by native farmers against the government took place in the 1910s and quickly led to the two-year-long Tauron Civil War. In the aftermath, government forces relied on urban units known as the Heraclis, whose wide-ranging powers in putting down a further rebellion led to the mass execution and starvation of many Taurons in an act recognized by the Twelve Worlds as a genocide, leading to a wide body of Ha'la'tha refugees settling across the Cyrannus star system from Caprica to Sagittaron, and became linked to organized crime. These planets' complicity in the regime's continued strength was not forgotten. In 1942 another uprising took place, though the Ha'la'tha was more prepared, and gun-running across the Twelve Worlds helped support the rebels.
Despite this Tauron was considered one of the richest and most industrious Colonies known for its pride, and vast wealth among the few.
The capital of Tauron is Hypatia, with other major cities being Tauron City and Minos.

### Virgon
"Imperial" Virgon is a temperate planet in the Cyrannus Star System, orbiting Helios Beta. It is the second largest of the habitable planets, smaller only than Leonis. It is named after the Virgo tribe who resided on Kobol, though its citizens may not have been affiliated with that tribe.
Following the conclusion of the Gemenese dark age and humanity's reinvention of space flight, Virgon was for a time one of the dominant powers in the star system, and formed the Virgon Empire which set up colonies on Libran; Picon; Sagittaron; Scorpia; Tauron, and Troy. The empire began a long decline approximately 850 years prior to the Cylon War, after a devastating war with the rival Leonese empire.
